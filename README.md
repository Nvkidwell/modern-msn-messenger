# 🖤⚡ DEADVOX — A Place For The Misunderstood

A raw, authentic 2000s post-hardcore / scene angst underground web messenger built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Express, and Socket.IO.

Zero corporate branding. Zero passwords. Zero posers. Completely silent (no audio).

---

## ✨ Features

- **No Sign-In Required (Instant Guest Access)**:
  - Join with zero passwords or registration forms.
  - Pick a custom handle, wire address (`@deadvox.net`), display picture, mood, and dramatic song lyrics.
  - **🎲 Roll Scene Persona**: One-click generator for iconic 2000s scene/emo handles (`crying_in_heavy_eyeliner`, `xX_ShadowOfTomorrow_Xx`, `rawr_means_iloveyou_xD`, `scenecore_royalty_06`).
  - Set your current mood: `🖤 Melancholy`, `⚡ Hyperactive (Rawr xD)`, `💀 Dead Inside`, `🌧️ Emo Tears`, `🎸 Mosh Pit Ready`, `✨ Glitter & Gloom`.

- **Completely Silent (No Sounds)**:
  - All sound effects and audio synthesizers have been stripped away as requested.
  - Retains all visual animations: violent screen shaking, particle blasts, animated emojis, and toast alerts with 0dB volume.

- **The Iconic SCREAM `(!)`**:
  - The scene reimagining of the classic messenger nudge: violently shakes everyone's screen with a dramatic red banner: `* Nicholas JUST SCREAMED AT YOU! *`.
  - Realistic cooldown timer to avoid overload.

- **Scene Blasts (Visual Screen Effects)**:
  - **Broken Heart Shatter (`💔`)**: Fractures a neon heart across the window into jagged shards!
  - **Rawr Dinosaur Blast (`🦖`)**: `xD RAWR means I love you in dinosaur!!`
  - **Bleeding Eyeliner (`👁️🖤`)**: Crying black mascara tears running down the screen.
  - **Static Shock (`⚡`)**: Flashing neon lightning bolt electrocutes the window.
  - **Toxic Skull (`☠️`)**: Poison green radioactive skull & crossbones.
  - **Glitter & Gloom (`✨`)**: Multi-colored scene neon glitter burst.

- **2000s Scene & Emo Emoticons**:
  - Live shortcut replacements: `xD`, `XD`, `>_<`, `T_T`, `x_x`, `-_-`, `O_o`, `(L)` black bleeding heart, `(U)` broken heart, `(6)` devil horns, `(X)` poison skull, `(V)` vampire bat, `(W)` wilted rose, `(K)` black lipstick kiss, `(R)` rawr dino, `(P)` safety pin, `(G)` black guitar, `(8)` screamo note.

- **Sharpie Scribble & Graffiti Canvas**:
  - Draw doodles, lyrics, and graffiti with high-contrast neon scene colors on a pitch-black canvas and post them directly into the chat.

- **Raven (Scene Queen Bot)**:
  - Pre-loaded AI companion who quotes Hawthorne Heights, My Chemical Romance, and Taking Back Sunday, asks who is in your Top 8, gives sarcastic emo advice, and **screams right back** when you hit SCREAM!

- **The Void**:
  - The public chat wall where anyone can drop in and post live with zero accounts.

## 🚀 How to Run

### Start the Server
```bash
# In the project directory:
npm start
```
Then open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your web browser.

### Test Multi-User Chat
1. Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in a browser tab.
2. Open another tab in Private/Incognito or another window.
3. Click **"🎲 Roll Scene Persona"** and hit **"Enter DEADVOX"**.
4. Both personas will appear on each other's Top 8 / Scene Kids buddy list and can chat, scream, and send scribbles in real-time!
